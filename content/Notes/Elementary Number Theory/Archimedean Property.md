### Archimedean Property of $\mathbb{N}$:

$\forall a,b\in\mathbb{N},\space\exists n\in\mathbb{N}\text{ s.t. }na\geq b$

Proof: We give a proof by contradiction.

$$ \text{Suppose that the statement is false. In other words, }\exists a,b\in\mathbb{N},\forall n\in\mathbb{N}\text{, such that }na<b.\text{ Let } X=\{b-na:n\in\mathbb{N} \}\text{ We know, from our assumption, that }X\subseteq\mathbb{N}.\text{ Because }X\subseteq\mathbb{N}\text{, and }X\neq\emptyset \text{ (for example }b-a\in X)\text{ the [[Well-Ordering-Principle]] says that }X \text{has a least element }m.$$
